Questions

How fast is it?

Within 24 hours of your order. We do our runs in the evening, which is the best time to put posters up.

How do I send you my poster?

Paste a Google Drive or Canva link at checkout, or send the file in Whop chat after you pay. If you chose Post only, we arrange a hand-over on campus.

Is this allowed on campus?

We only use noticeboards and follow DCU's Notice Board Regulations. If your poster needs a stamp from your School or Unit before it goes up, we tell you before we post.

What if a board is full?

We never cover someone else's current poster. We use the nearest board with space and tell you which one.

Do you take the posters down?

Yes. Give us a take-down date and we remove them, so the boards stay tidy for the next event.

Who is behind Poster Pal?

Two DCU computer science students. We spent an evening putting up our own research posters and decided nobody else should have to.
